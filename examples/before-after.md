# Example: revise the sentence, not just the words

## Before

Nestled in the heart of the city, our café stands as a testament to the town's rich cultural heritage. Additionally, the menu boasts a diverse array of pastries, showcasing our commitment to quality. While specific details about opening hours are not widely documented, it's important to note that hours may vary.

## After, using only supported information

Our café is in the city. The menu includes pastries. Opening hours are not confirmed, so check before visiting.

## Why this edit works

The original version adds praise without evidence, uses promotional wording, and repeats uncertainty in a padded way. The revision removes those patterns without inventing details about the café's location, history, menu size, or opening hours.

A real rewrite should use any additional facts supplied by the author. Never invent a founding date, number of products, social media account, or other detail just to make the prose sound more specific.
